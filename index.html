1. POSITION AND DUTIES

The Employer agrees to employ the Employee in the capacity of [Designation]. The Employee shall perform all duties as assigned by the management from time to time and shall report to [Reporting Manager].

2. COMPENSATION

The Employee shall receive a Cost to Company (CTC) of ₹ [Monthly CTC] per month, subject to applicable tax deductions under the Income Tax Act, 1961. Salary shall be credited on or before the 7th working day of every month.

3. PROBATION PERIOD
